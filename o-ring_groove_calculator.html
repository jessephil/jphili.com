Face seal glands are dimensioned differently from the radial grooves above — by the groove's outer diameter (for external pressure) or inner diameter (for internal pressure), each sized directly off the O-ring's mean OD or ID rather than a bore/rod diameter with a diametral clearance. This chart is included for reference only; it isn't wired into the calculator above since it needs that different dimensioning approach, not just a different squeeze target.

Method

Squeeze (%) = (CS − groove depth) / CS × 100 Gland fill (%) = O-ring cross-section area / groove cross-section area × 100, arearing = π(CS/2)²

Static seals use Parker ORD 5703 Design Chart II (Industrial Static Seals — the same data appears in the Parker O-Ring Handbook, ORD 5700, as Design Chart 4-2), which tabulates squeeze percentage, gland depth, and groove width directly for each of the five standard AS568 cross-sections (0.070", 0.103", 0.139", 0.210", 0.275"). This calculator uses the midpoint of Parker's stated range at each of those five cross-sections and linearly interpolates between them for in-between sizes.

Reciprocating seals now use Parker ORD 5703 Design Chart III (Industrial Reciprocating Seals, 1500 psi max) the same way — tabulated squeeze at the five standard cross-sections, interpolated between them. Groove width reuses Chart II's column, since ORD 5703 tabulates the same groove width for both static and reciprocating glands at a given cross-section.

Rotary seals are the one application here without a manufacturer chart to draw on — ORD 5703 doesn't include one. This calculator falls back to commonly cited industry guidance (5–10% squeeze, ~1.5× CS groove width) for rotary only. Treat that one as a starting point, not a substitute for your seal supplier's dynamic gland spec.

CS = O-ring cross-section (bulk) diameter ID = O-ring inner diameter Groove width/area treated as rectangular (corner radii ignored) Target gland fill: ~75–90% (avoid >90% — risks hydraulic lock/over-compression)

Sizes: SAE/ISO AS568 standard O-ring dimensions (inch series, dash 001–475). Squeeze/groove data for static and reciprocating seals: Parker O-Ring Dimension & Seal Material Reference Guide, ORD 5703 (Design Charts II and III); the static numbers also appear in the Parker O-Ring Handbook, ORD 5700, Design Chart 4-2. Rotary squeeze/groove guidance: general industry practice, not a tabulated manufacturer chart. Cross-sections below 0.070" (dash 001–003) fall outside ORD 5703's gland charts and are clamped to the 0.070" values with a note — verify independently for those minis. This tool doesn't account for pressure-driven extrusion gaps, back-up rings, temperature de-rating, or fluid compatibility swell — check those separately for your application.
