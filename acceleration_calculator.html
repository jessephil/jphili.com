Drivetrain Acceleration

Wheel torque = Engine torque × gear ratio × diff ratio × η Wheel force (lbs) = Wheel torque (ft·lb) / tire radius (ft) Acceleration (m/s²) = Wheel force (N) / total mass (kg) g = acceleration / 9.81 Tire radius (in) = rim_dia/2 + tire_width(mm) × aspect/100 / 25.4 HP ↔ Torque: T (ft·lb) = HP × 5252 / RPM
η = drivetrain efficiency Diff ratio = axle / differential gear ratio Final ratio = gear ratio × diff ratio 1 ft·lb = 1.35582 N·m 1 lbf = 4.44822 N
