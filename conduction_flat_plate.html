Fourier's Law — Flat Plate

Q = k · A · ΔT / X
Q = heat transfer rate (W) k = thermal conductivity (W/m·K) A = cross-sectional area (m²) ΔT = temperature difference (K or °C) X = plate thickness (m) R (SI) = X / (k·A) — thermal resistance (K/W) R (imperial) = Xft / kBTU — insulation R-value (hr·ft²·°F/BTU)
