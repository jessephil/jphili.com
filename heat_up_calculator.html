Sensible Heat Equation

Q = m · Cp · ΔT P = Q / t   [required power] t = Q / P   [required time] P_actual = P_ideal / η   [accounting for efficiency]
Q = heat energy (kJ) m = mass (kg) Cp = specific heat capacity (kJ/kg·K) ΔT = temperature rise (K or °C) P = power (kW) t = time (s) η = system efficiency Cp for oils evaluated at mean temperature (T₁+T₂)/2
