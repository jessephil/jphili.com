Power Screw Torque-Force Equation (Machinery's Handbook)

F = 2π · T / (p + 2π · µ · r) σ = F / Aₜ   [bolt tensile stress] F_proof = Sp · Aₜ   [proof load] Preload % = F / F_proof × 100 Aₜ (in²) = 0.7854 · (D − 0.9743/n)²   [UNC/UNF stress area] Aₜ (mm²) = 0.7854 · (D − 0.9382·p)²   [metric stress area]
T = applied torque p = thread pitch (in or mm) µ = coefficient of friction (thread + bearing face) r = D/2 = nominal radius Aₜ = tensile stress area Sp = proof strength (material-dependent) Typical µ: 0.10 lubricated, 0.15 dry steel, 0.20 rough/corroded
