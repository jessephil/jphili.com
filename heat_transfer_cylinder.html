Fourier's Law — Hollow Cylinder (Radial Conduction)

Q = 2π · k · L · ΔT / ln(ro / rᵢ)
Q = heat transfer rate (W) k = thermal conductivity (W/m·K) L = cylinder length (m) ΔT = Ti − To (K or °C) rᵢ = inner radius (m) ro = outer radius (m) R = ln(ro/rᵢ) / (2π·k·L) — thermal resistance (K/W)
