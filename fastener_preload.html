VDI 2230 / Shigley Full Torque Equation

T = F · [ p/(2π) + µₜ · rₜ + µᵦ · rᵦ ] rₜ = (D − 0.6495·p) / 2   [mean thread radius, 60° thread] rᵦ = Dₕ / 2   [effective bearing radius ≈ washer/head OD / 2] K = T / (F · D)   [nut factor — validates T = K·D·F] F_proof = Sp · Aₜ   |   σ = F / Aₜ
µₜ = thread friction coefficient µᵦ = bearing face friction coefficient rₜ = mean thread radius (in) rᵦ = effective bearing radius (in) K = nut factor (typically 0.15–0.22) Sp = proof strength Aₜ = tensile stress area
