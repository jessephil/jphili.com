Lamé Equations — Thick-Walled Cylinder

σθ = P·rᵢ² / (ro²−rᵢ²) · (1 + ro²/r²)  [tangential] σr = P·rᵢ² / (ro²−rᵢ²) · (1 − ro²/r²)  [radial] σL = P·rᵢ² / (ro²−rᵢ²)  [longitudinal, closed ends] σVM = √(σθ²+σr²+σL²−σθσr−σθσL−σrσL)  [Von Mises]
P = internal pressure rᵢ = inner radius ro = outer radius r = radius at point of interest Sᵧ = material yield strength (for safety factor)
